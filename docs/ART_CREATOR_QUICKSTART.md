# Quick Start: Art Creator

## 3 Steps to Create Your First Artwork

### 1. Navigate to Actions
Go to the GitHub repository → Click **Actions** tab

### 2. Select Art Creator
- Click **Art Creator** in the left sidebar
- Click the **Run workflow** dropdown button

### 3. Choose Your Parameters
Pick from the dropdown menus:

**Quick Presets:**

#### 🌅 Relaxing Sunset
```
Art Period: impressionist
Visual Pattern: particle_flow
Color Palette: sunset
Music Style: gamelan
Duration: 10min
```

#### ⚡ Energizing Focus
```
Art Period: cyberpunk
Visual Pattern: fractal_zoom
Color Palette: psychedelic
Music Style: burundi
Duration: 15min
```

#### 🌙 Sleep Meditation
```
Art Period: impressionist
Visual Pattern: starfield
Color Palette: ocean
Music Style: heartbeat
Duration: 30min
```

### 4. Run and Download
- Click **Run workflow** at the bottom
- Wait for completion (check the Actions page)
- Download your creation from the Artifacts section

## Tips

- **Start short**: Use 5min duration for testing
- **Save seeds**: Each run shows a seed in the summary - save it to recreate later
- **Experiment**: No combination is "wrong" - explore freely!
- **Share**: Share your seed and parameters with others

## What Makes Each Creation Unique?

Every parameter combination creates a unique video:
- **9 art periods** × **7 patterns** × **11 palettes** × **9 rhythms** = **Over 6,000 base combinations**
- Add in speed, complexity, tempo, frequencies = **Millions of possibilities**
- Each seed generates a unique variation

## Philosophy

This is your digital canvas. Like an artist mixing paints:
- No "right" or "wrong" choices
- The joy is in the creation process
- Every result is unique
- Share your creations with the world
